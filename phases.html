Keep the article.

A Firefox extension on Android and desktop. Click Know on the current page to save its URL, title, and extracted article HTML to your application server.

Check the outcome

Server confirms storage. Green success transitions immediately to blue saved; red means error. Initial and in-progress appearances are visual proposals below.

What is stored: URL + title + Readability's extracted article HTML, submitted as a string and stored in PostgreSQL TEXT. This preserves extracted structure, not the whole original page. Text-capture failure permits URL/title-only saving. Content exceeding 4 MiB is rejected with a message, without fallback.

Capture-state behavior
  • Save: one click captures and sends immediately, with no review step. Confirm success only after the server responds; request timeout is 60 seconds.
  • Duplicate: exact URL matching. Ask Update existing / Cancel. Automatically fill missing content, but never replace existing content without explicit user instruction.
  • Error: a missing confirmation does not prove that nothing was stored. The user retries from the article; no stored queue or special retry deduplication. Existing duplicate rules apply.

Preview only: all controls above switch prepared illustrations. No page is read or saved. Quiet utility is selected. Ready/saving colors remain proposals. Saved is blue; errors are red. Green success transitions immediately to blue, so no separate green resting screen is shown.

4 · Grow from capture into memory.

The order below is a discussion proposal, not an implementation commitment. Each increment requires a scope decision before moving forward.

Proposed phase 2

Manage & find saved discoveries

Build on the Phase 1 list and reader with item management and basic title or keyword finding.

Proposed phase 3

Understand & organize

Introduce rich previews, transparent processing states, suggested topics, and summaries where content is available. Let users correct suggestions.

Proposed phase 4

Connect sources to a personal library

Bring in books, movies, and other entities; track intentions and activities; explore discovery and consumption history separately.

Proposed phase 5

Explore & rediscover

Add meaningful graph exploration, natural-language retrieval, and user-requested insights grounded in saved sources.

What capture channels remain outside Phase 1?

Firefox Android and desktop are both included in Phase 1. iOS, other browsers, and dedicated deferred-restricted-content workflows remain outside the confirmed first increment.

Privacy and personal-data control apply throughout; they are not deferred to the final increment. The appropriate first-release scope remains to be agreed.
